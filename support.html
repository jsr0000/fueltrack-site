FAQs

How does FuelTrack use my Apple Health data?

It reads your completed workouts, active energy, heart rate, step count, and body weight to detect training and set your daily macro targets. That processing happens on your device, and your Health history is never uploaded. When a workout produces an AI insight, a short summary of that single workout — type, duration, distance, average and maximum heart rate, calories — is sent with the request. The Privacy & Data page lists every field, and what is never sent.

Can I use FuelTrack without Apple Health?

Yes. If you skip Health access, FuelTrack sets your macro targets from your training plan instead of your actual workouts. You can connect Apple Health at any time from the Profile tab.

Do I need a subscription?

You get 7 days of full access when you finish onboarding, with no payment method required. After that, a subscription is required to keep using the app — there is no free tier. Prices and trial terms are shown on the subscription screen before you buy. See the Terms of Use.

How do I log food?

On the Fuel tab, open a meal slot and choose how to log it: scan a barcode, take a photo, type a description, or say it out loud. Barcode results come from the Open Food Facts database; photo, text, and voice are estimated by AI. Every estimate is editable.

Why are my macro estimates off?

Photo, text, and voice logging produce estimates, and portion size is the hardest part to judge from a description or a picture. Tap a logged item to correct any value. Barcode scanning is exact where the product exists in Open Food Facts.

How do I change my goal or dietary preferences?

Everything is on the Profile tab — stats, fitness goal, training type and race goal, dietary preferences, and allergies. Changing something that affects your macro targets prompts you to regenerate the current week; otherwise the change applies from next week.

How do I delete my data?

Use Delete Account on the Profile tab, or in the footer of the subscription screen. It immediately and permanently removes your profile, training plan, fuel log, logged food photos, and stored credentials from the device, and it cannot be undone. Deleting the app also removes all local data. Section 8 of the Privacy & Data page explains the one thing deliberately kept — the date your free trial started, so the trial cannot be restarted.

My workout didn't show up

FuelTrack picks up workouts from Apple Health, so first check the workout is there — anything from Garmin, Strava, Apple Watch, or Whoop has to sync into Apple Health first. Then open FuelTrack, which catches up on the last two days when it comes to the foreground. If it is in Apple Health and still missing, email us with the date and workout type.
