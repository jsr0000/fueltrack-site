FuelTrack Privacy & Data

Last updated: 2 October 2026

FuelTrack ("we", "our", "the app") is a training and nutrition app for iOS. It reads your workouts from Apple Health, turns them into daily macro targets, and lets you log what you eat. This page is the complete account of what data the app handles, what leaves your device, and what your rights are.

  • Your data lives on your device. We do not run an account database and hold no profile for you.
  • Your Apple Health history never leaves your device.
  • The AI features send the relevant text, photo, or numbers to Anthropic's Claude API through our proxy. No identity is attached — only a random device token.
  • Usage analytics you can switch off, identified only by a random code created on your device — never your name, email, or Apple ID — plus measurements of how the AI features perform that carry no identifier at all. No tracking, no advertising, no data sold or shared.

1. Data stored on your device only

The following never leaves your device except where §5 says otherwise. It is held in the app's private storage and, for credentials, the iOS Keychain:

  • Profile — name, age, height, weight, sex, dietary preferences, allergies, fitness goal, profile photo
  • Training plan — your generated schedule, session completion history, and adaptive adjustments
  • Fuel log — daily macro targets, every food item you log, and any photos you take to log food
  • App preferences — appearance, units, and notification settings
  • Credentials — your Apple user identifier and a random device token, both in the Keychain

2. Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, FuelTrack reads the following from Apple Health. It never writes to Apple Health.

  • Active energy burned
  • Workouts — type, duration, distance
  • Heart rate — average and maximum per workout
  • Step count
  • Body weight

This is used to detect completed workouts and to set your daily macro targets. Processing happens on your device. Your Health history is never uploaded, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never disclosed to data brokers. When a workout triggers an AI insight, a short summary of that single workout is included in the request — see §5.

3. Sign in with Apple

Signing in with Apple is required to use FuelTrack. We receive your Apple user identifier and, if you allow it, your name. We do not request your email address. The identifier is stored in your device's Keychain, is never sent to our servers, and is never included in any AI request.

4. Camera and barcode scanning

With your permission, FuelTrack uses your camera to scan product barcodes. The barcode number alone is sent to the Open Food Facts API to look up the product name and nutrition. No personal data, health data, or device identifier is included, and no image is transmitted or stored. Photos you take to log food by picture are stored locally and are sent only as described in §5.

5. AI features — exactly what is sent

These features use Anthropic's Claude API: food logging by text, photo, or voice; the post-workout insight; the morning briefing; rest-day guidance; the weekly coaching summary; and race-day nutrition guidance.

Requests go over HTTPS to our own proxy server (a Cloudflare Worker), which holds the API key and forwards the request to Anthropic. The app itself never holds the key.

Sent

The food description, photo, or voice transcription you submit To identify the food and estimate its macros
Body weight and fitness goal To calibrate portion and macro estimates
Today's or this week's macro targets and what you logged against them, including food names To write feedback on how your fuelling landed
A summary of one completed workout — type, duration, distance, average and maximum heart rate, calories burned To write the post-workout insight and the morning briefing

Your first name, where an insight addresses you directly So the briefing reads as written for you

Never sent

  • Your Apple Health history — only the single-workout summary above, and only when an insight is generated
  • Location or GPS data — never collected by the app at all
  • Your Apple user identifier or email address
  • Your profile photo
  • Your photo library, contacts, or calendar — only the one image you choose to log

Voice logging is transcribed on your device by Apple's speech framework. Only the resulting text is sent; the audio never leaves your device.

How Anthropic handles it

Data sent through Anthropic's API is not used to train their models. Requests are processed and discarded. See anthropic.com/privacy for their full policy.

If the AI is unavailable

Every one of these features has an on-device, rule-based fallback built from the same data. When the API is unreachable, the app falls back to it — nothing is queued or retried later against your will.

6. Subscription and free trial

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details, card, or billing address.

So that the 7-day free trial cannot be restarted by deleting and reinstalling the app, the date your trial started is recorded in three places: on your device, in your own iCloud key-value storage, and against your random device token on our proxy server. Our server also keeps a monthly count of AI requests per device token, as a cost ceiling.

That timestamp and that counter are the only data we hold against your device token. Neither contains your name, email, Apple identifier, health data, training plan, or food log. The trial record expires 90 days after it is written; the usage counter expires after 62 days. The usage analytics and service measurements in §7 are held separately and carry no device token.

7. Usage analytics and service measurements

Usage analytics. To learn which features help, the app sends usage events to our own server — for example "a meal was logged by barcode", "the app was opened today", "onboarding reached the goal step", or "the subscription screen was closed without a purchase". Each event carries the app version, how many days ago the app was installed, and a random identifier created on your device.

That identifier is not connected to your Apple ID, name, email, or device token, and is replaced if you delete your account. Because it is a persistent identifier, privacy law treats these events as personal data, and our App Store privacy label lists them as linked to you — but we hold nothing that would let us work out who you are from them. We never send what you ate, your macros, body stats, training plan, Health data, or anything you type. Events are kept for up to three months. Turn usage analytics off at any time in Profile → Settings → Share Usage Analytics.

Service measurements. Each time an AI feature runs, our server records what kind of request it was (for example "text food log" or "daily insight"), which Claude model answered, how many tokens it used, how long it took, and whether it succeeded. This carries no identifier of any kind and none of the content sent or returned. We use it to keep the AI features fast, reliable, and affordable. Because it describes our service rather than you, it is not affected by the analytics setting above. It is kept for up to three months.

8. Data we do not collect

  • No tracking — nothing links what you do in FuelTrack with data from other companies' apps or websites
  • No advertising or attribution SDKs, and no advertising identifier
  • No location data
  • No cookies or web tracking
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties

9. Retention and deletion

Delete Account — in the app, under Profile, or from the footer of the subscription screen. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, training plan, fuel log, logged food photos, your Apple user identifier, and your device token from the device. It cannot be undone.

Deleting the app removes all local data with it.

One exception, stated plainly: the trial start date described in §6 is deliberately retained so the free trial cannot be restarted. It is a single date, holds nothing personal, and expires on the schedule above. Clearing your device token also orphans the server-side records, which then expire on their own.

10. Children's privacy

FuelTrack is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Not medical advice

FuelTrack estimates macro targets and food macros to support training. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical or clinical nutrition advice. AI-generated macro estimates are estimates — you can edit any of them. Talk to a qualified professional before making significant changes to your diet or training, particularly if you have a medical condition.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. Changes are posted here with an updated date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Because your data is held on your device, you exercise all three directly in the app at any time. For the trial timestamp and usage counter held against your device token, email us and we will delete them. The usage analytics in §7 carry only a random identifier we cannot connect to your name or email, so we cannot look them up from either; you can stop them at any time in Settings, deleting your account replaces the identifier, and events expire after three months.
